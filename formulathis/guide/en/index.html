How can I create and export math formulas with formulaThis?

01 — CREATE

Enter your formula

Use the visual editor to build your formula with shortcuts and mathematical symbols, or enter your LaTeX code directly.

x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}
02 — PREVIEW

See the result instantly

Your formula is rendered immediately. You can adjust your input and check the result before exporting it.

03 — EXPORT

Export your formula

Once your formula is ready, use the export options to use it in documents, presentations, lessons or social media posts.

PNG

A high-resolution image, with or without background.

Copy

Quickly copy your formula to the clipboard.

PDF

Export your formula as a vector PDF.
