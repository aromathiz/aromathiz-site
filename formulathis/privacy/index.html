Privacy Policy – formulaThis

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Introduction

formulaThis respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

This privacy policy explains what information may be collected when you use the application, why it is processed, which third-party services may be involved, and what rights you may have.

Data Collected

Depending on your consent and the configuration of the services used, formulaThis may collect technical and usage data through Google Firebase services.

This data may include:

  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • Technical information about your device, such as its model, operating system version, and app version.
  • Application usage data and interactions with features, such as information used to understand how the application is used.
  • Technical information necessary to diagnose errors and improve application stability.

Firebase Analytics may process app usage and interaction data to help us understand how the application is used and improve its features.

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash reports and technical diagnostic information to help identify and fix errors.

We do not intentionally collect the contents of your mathematical documents through Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics for analytics or diagnostic purposes.

Purpose of Data Processing

Where applicable and permitted by your consent, technical and usage data may be processed to:

  • Detect and fix bugs.
  • Improve application stability and performance.
  • Understand how application features are used.
  • Improve the application's usability and reliability.

Advertising

formulaThis does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services such as Google AdMob.

We do not use your information to display targeted advertisements.

In-App Purchases

formulaThis offers in-app purchases that allow users to unlock premium features.

Payments are processed by Google Play through Google Play Billing. The application does not collect or store your payment card or banking details.

Google Play may process purchase-related information as necessary to complete and manage transactions. This processing is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

formulaThis uses Google services to support application diagnostics, usage analysis, and in-app purchases:

  • Firebase Analytics: processes application usage and interaction data to help understand how the application is used and improve its features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: processes crash reports and technical diagnostic information to help identify and fix errors and improve application stability.
  • Google Play Billing: processes and manages in-app purchases.

These services may process technical information in accordance with their configuration, applicable consent settings, and relevant data protection laws.

For more information about Google's data processing practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent and Your Choices

Where required, the application asks for your consent before collecting optional analytics or diagnostic data. Your choice is handled according to the consent options and settings implemented in the application.

You can decline optional data collection where the application provides that choice. Where supported, you may also be able to change your preferences through the application's settings.

Declining optional analytics or diagnostic collection does not necessarily disable all technical processing associated with the application or Google Play purchases. Any processing necessary to provide a requested service or otherwise permitted by applicable law is handled separately, where applicable.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Information may be processed by the third-party service providers identified in this policy for the purposes described above. These providers may process data on their own infrastructure in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising.

Data Retention

Data retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the third-party service involved. Information is retained according to the applicable service settings, retention policies, and legal requirements.

For information about retention periods for data processed by Google Firebase, please consult Google's documentation and privacy policy.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed through the application and its service providers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to data portability or to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To ask a question about privacy or exercise a right relating to data for which we are responsible, contact us using the address below.

Requests concerning data processed directly by a third-party provider may also need to be addressed to that provider.

Children's Privacy

formulaThis is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary for the application's functionality and the services described in this policy. Where applicable, data processing is subject to the consent requirements and protections provided by law.
